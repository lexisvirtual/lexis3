4. **Substantial**: "This project will bring **substantial** benefits."

## Erros Comuns | Avoiding Amateur Pitfalls
Evite frases genéricas que não transmitem autoridade. Use expressões de impacto para se destacar.

| Don't say | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| "We have a problem." | "We are **addressing** a **critical** issue." |
| "I need more time." | "I am **stalling for time** to ensure accuracy." |
